RESEARCH ASSISTANT PROFESSOR

Dehao Xu

Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering
The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

My research interests span complex compressible transitional and turbulent wall-bounded flows, multiphase incompressible flows, and machine-learning-assisted modelling. I seek to understand the physics of compressible and incompressible flows and use these physical insights to develop advanced machine-learning models.

I received my Ph.D. in Fluid Mechanics from Peking University in 2023 under the supervision of Prof. Shiyi Chen, following a B.Sc. from the Southern University of Science and Technology in 2018. I then carried out postdoctoral research at the University of Twente with Prof. Detlef Lohse and at PolyU with Prof. Chih-Yung Wen.

I am currently a Research Assistant Professor at PolyU.
