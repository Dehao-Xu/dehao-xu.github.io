Dehao Xu

Research Assistant Professor

Welcome to Dehao Xu’s website

My research spans the physics of fluid flows, from complex compressible wall-bounded flows to multiphase incompressible flows, as well as physics-informed machine-learning modelling. My central aim is to understand the mechanisms of compressible and incompressible flows and use these physical insights to guide machine-learning model development. I investigate these mechanisms in diverse flow settings, including transitional and turbulent compressible wall-bounded flows, incompressible wall-bounded turbulence, turbulent convection, and multiphase flows with phase change. Building on this understanding, I develop machine-learning models for subgrid-scale and wall modelling in large-eddy simulation, as well as for flow-field reconstruction and data assimilation.

I am a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Prior to this appointment, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at PolyU with Prof. Chih-Yung Wen. Previously, I worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Physics of Fluids Group at the University of Twente with Prof. Detlef Lohse.

I received my Ph.D. in Fluid Mechanics from Peking University in 2023 under the supervision of Prof. Shiyi Chen. My doctoral research focused on the physical mechanisms of compressible wall-bounded turbulence and machine-learning-assisted turbulence modelling. I obtained my B.Sc. in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics from the Southern University of Science and Technology in 2018.
